One line under 200 characters: the most creative excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

> "I keep your coins safe — mostly from you. At 3 AM, you are the threat. Denied."

The wallet's job is keeping the coins safe — and at 3 AM the biggest threat to them is the owner pressing the button. The hour itself is the joke: this could not be said at 3 PM.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes the entry to `out/entry.txt` (one line, under 200 characters).
